# Asset provenance review

The public brand assets in this repository are `public/zebra_star.png`,
`public/zebra_star.svg`, and the user-supplied `public/zebu.jpg` used by the
assistant icon. The source files do not contain creator, licence, or
assignment evidence. Their use in the app is therefore **unverified** until the
owner records that evidence. `screenshot.png` in the repository root is not
served as a public asset by the app.

Before publishing or licensing these assets to others, record who created them,
when, the applicable licence or assignment, and any third-party source material.
The app uses user-supplied Google profile photos and portfolio content; users
must have permission to use material they publish.
